TOUCH CONTROLS

Use the left pad to move and the right cluster to attack. Every button supports simultaneous multi-touch, so you can jump and strike at once.

  • LIGHT is fast and chains into combos.
  • HEAVY is slow but launches and breaks guards.
  • SPECIAL is a character projectile with a short cooldown.
  • SUPER spends a full meter for a cinematic attack.
  • GUARD blocks high and low; blocking still takes chip damage.
  • Double-tap left or right on the pad to dash.

FIGHTING SYSTEM

  • Land light attacks to start combos — light chains into light, heavy or special.
  • Damage scales down as combos get longer, so no infinite loops.
  • Your super meter fills when you deal or take damage.
  • Round timer runs out: the healthier fighter wins the round.
  • First to two rounds wins the match. Boss fights give King Tomato three phases.
  • Pause any time with the pause button or Escape.
